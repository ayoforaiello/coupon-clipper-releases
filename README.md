# Coupon Clipper

A small app that clips every digital coupon onto your grocery loyalty card, for everyone in your household, with one click. Made by Antonio Aiello for family.

## Download

| Computer | File |
|---|---|
| **Windows** 10 or 11 | [Coupon-Clipper-Windows-Setup.exe](https://github.com/ayoforaiello/coupon-clipper-releases/releases/latest/download/Coupon-Clipper-Windows-Setup.exe) |
| **Mac** with Apple chip (M1 or newer) | [Coupon-Clipper-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip](https://github.com/ayoforaiello/coupon-clipper-releases/releases/latest/download/Coupon-Clipper-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip) |
| **Mac** with Intel chip | [Coupon-Clipper-Mac-Intel.zip](https://github.com/ayoforaiello/coupon-clipper-releases/releases/latest/download/Coupon-Clipper-Mac-Intel.zip) |

Not sure which Mac? Apple menu > About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M..." means Apple chip; "Processor: Intel" means Intel.

**You also need Google Chrome** ([google.com/chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/)). The sign-in happens in Chrome.

## Install

**Windows**
1. Open the downloaded file.
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. (This shows for apps from small developers.)
3. Click through the installer. Coupon Clipper opens, and an icon goes on your Desktop.

**Mac**
1. Open the downloaded zip. Drag **Coupon Clipper** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Open it. The first time, the Mac says it can't verify the app. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Coupon Clipper. Confirm with your password. You only do this once.

## Use

1. Type a name under **Add someone** and click **Add & sign in**. Chrome opens your store's page. Sign in the normal way, once. The app never sees your password.
2. Click **Clip everything for everyone**.
3. Add more people the same way. Each person signs in to their own account.

New versions show an **Update ready** bar in the app. Click **Update now**. Your sign-ins carry over.
